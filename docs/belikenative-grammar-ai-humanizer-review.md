## The Problem: L1 Interference in English Writing {#the-problem}

Every non-native English speaker carries invisible grammar habits from their mother tongue. Linguists call this L1 interference or language transfer. These are not random mistakes — they are systematic patterns that standard grammar checkers cannot detect because the sentences are often grammatically valid, just unnatural.

### Common L1 interference examples:

- **Article errors** (common for speakers of Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Polish): "I went to store" instead of "I went to the store"
- **Preposition confusion** (common for speakers of Spanish, Portuguese, French, German): "I am interested on this" instead of "I am interested in this"
- **Word order shifts** (common for speakers of German, Dutch, Japanese, Turkish): "I yesterday went to the meeting" instead of "I went to the meeting yesterday"
- **Tense misuse** (common for speakers of Mandarin, Thai, Vietnamese): "I go to the store yesterday" instead of "I went to the store yesterday"
- **False cognates** (common for speakers of Spanish, Italian, French): Using "actually" to mean "currently" because the word looks similar in their language

These errors are invisible to tools like Grammarly because they focus on universal English grammar rules, not language-specific transfer patterns. BeLikeNative is designed from the ground up to detect and correct these patterns based on your specific native language.

## Key Features {#features}

### L1-Aware Grammar Correction

BeLikeNative asks you to set your native language when you first install it. From that point on, every correction is tailored to the specific error patterns speakers of your language tend to make. A Russian speaker gets different corrections than a Japanese speaker, because they make different types of mistakes.

The extension supports over 80 native languages, covering the vast majority of the world's non-native English speakers.

### AI Text Humanizer

One of BeLikeNative's standout features is its AI humanizer. If you use ChatGPT, Claude, or any other AI tool to draft text, you know the output often sounds robotic — overly formal, repetitive in structure, and peppered with telltale AI phrases like "It is important to note that" or "In today's fast-paced world."

BeLikeNative's humanizer, powered by Claude Sonnet 4.6, rewrites AI-generated text to sound like it was written by a real person. It preserves the meaning and information while adjusting tone, sentence structure, and word choice to match natural human writing patterns.

### 1,100+ Custom Prompt Templates

BeLikeNative includes over 1,100 pre-built prompt templates for common writing scenarios:

- Professional emails
- LinkedIn posts and comments
- Cover letters and resumes
- Academic writing
- Slack and Teams messages
- Social media content
- Customer support responses

Each template is optimized for the specific context and can be customized further.

### Works on Every Website

Unlike some writing tools that only work in specific text editors or platforms, BeLikeNative works on every website. Gmail, LinkedIn, Slack, Google Docs, Twitter, Reddit, Discord — anywhere you type, BeLikeNative is available.

### Keyboard Shortcuts

Power users can trigger corrections and humanization with keyboard shortcuts, making the workflow seamless. No need to click buttons or navigate menus — just select text and press a shortcut.
